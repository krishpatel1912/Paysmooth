Problem Statement: Income Volatility for Indian Gig Workers

The Problem

India's gig economy powers urban delivery, but gig workers live in constant financial uncertainty:

Income Volatility: Earnings fluctuate unpredictably from day to day due to demand spikes, weather changes, algorithm shifts, vehicle maintenance, or illness.

Fixed Financial Commitments: While daily earnings are unpredictable, fixed costs remain rigid—monthly house rent, vehicle loan EMIs, phone recharges, and family needs must be paid on time.

The Debt Trap: A single bad week or a few missed workdays often forces delivery riders to borrow money through high-interest informal loans or pay late penalties on EMIs.

Target User Persona: Ravi

Profile: 26-year-old Swiggy delivery rider operating in Nashik, Maharashtra.

Earnings: Ranges between ₹400 to ₹1,200/day depending on orders, fuel costs, and hours worked.

Monthly Fixed Commitments:

Room Rent: ₹6,000

Bike Loan EMI: ₹3,500

Phone Data/Recharge: ₹400

Total Fixed Monthly Commitments: ₹9,900

Primary Pain Point: On high-earning days, Ravi overspends without realizing that upcoming lean days will leave him short on his fixed bike EMI or house rent payment.

Scale & Opportunity

Market Size: Over 7.7+ million gig workers currently in India, projected to expand to 23.5 million by 2030.

Core Segment: Delivery partners across platforms like Swiggy, Zomato, Zepto, Blinkit, and Shadowfax.

Impact: PaySmooth eliminates income stress by providing income-smoothing algorithms, AI financial coaching in native languages, and real-time shortfall simulation to keep workers financially resilient.
