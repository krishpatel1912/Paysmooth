PaySmooth - 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Pitch Script

[0:00 - 0:45] The Hook & Problem

"Meet Ravi. He’s a Swiggy delivery rider in Nashik. On Monday he makes ₹1,100, on Tuesday ₹850, but on Wednesday his bike breaks down and he makes zero.

At the end of the month, his room rent of ₹6,000 and bike EMI of ₹3,500 don't care that he had two bad weeks. Like millions of Indian gig workers, Ravi suffers from acute income volatility. When earnings are unpredictable, daily cash is spent instantly, leaving fixed liabilities at risk.

Today, gig workers resort to high-interest local money lenders or late penalty fees just to bridge a 3-day income gap."

[0:45 - 1:45] The Solution (PaySmooth Demo)

"Introducing PaySmooth—an AI-powered income-smoothing app designed specifically for Indian gig workers.

Let's look at Ravi's live dashboard:

Automated Weekly Bucketing: PaySmooth automatically breaks down Ravi's daily income into weekly automated reserves—ring-fencing his rent and EMI first, before showing him his Safe Daily Spendable Limit (e.g., ₹320/day).

7-Day Income Forecast: Using historical payout patterns, PaySmooth forecasts Ravi’s next 7 days of income so he can plan ahead.

AI Coach in Native Language: Powered by Gemini 2.0 Flash, Ravi gets real-time, actionable coaching in his preferred language—Marathi or Hindi. Look at this prompt response: 'Hi Ravi! Keep ₹200 aside today for your upcoming EMI and limit today's spending to ₹300.'"

[1:45 - 2:30] Key Feature: Skip-Day Stress Simulator

"What happens if Ravi gets sick or wants to visit home for 3 days?

With our Shortfall Simulator, Ravi can select 'Skip 3 Days'. PaySmooth recalculates his adjusted weekly earnings instantly, flags if his bike EMI is at risk, and calculates how much of his emergency bucket will cushion the drop.

He gets immediate clarity before taking time off, preventing debt traps before they happen."

[2:30 - 3:00] Business Value & Vision

"PaySmooth requires zero complex financial setups or stock market investments—we offer no investment advice, strictly focus on cash flow protection and budgeting.

Our architecture is API-ready to plug directly into Swiggy, Zomato, or Zepto worker apps as a value-added financial wellness module.

PaySmooth turns volatile daily paychecks into a smooth, predictable life for India's 7 million gig heroes. Thank you!"
